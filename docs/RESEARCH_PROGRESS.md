# Evidence-linked research progress

CRPN retains every accepted Fact and its actual predecessor lineage. A research
assessment is a separate, **unverified Selector judgment** about what those
results change: the continuing series, established interfaces and conditions,
latest delta, covered work, method limitations, and next unanswered question.
There is no progress score, automatic mathematical implication, or extra agent.

The ordinary Selector records `research_assessment` in its existing confirmed
selection. Its Fact references must have appeared as complete source interfaces
in that decision's material pages. Coverage and limitation relations cite those
references and remain revisable judgments. They neither merge Facts nor discharge
Claims. A null assessment honestly records that none was formed. Different
constructions and assumptions must be compared explicitly; later registration,
a larger endpoint, and predecessor links do not mechanically imply coverage.

The next exposure derives `research_progress` from the last serviced selection
of each already exposed Study, using the existing `last_served` journal. Older
selections without the field retain their actual action text, not invented
retrospective assessments. Revoked or missing sources invalidate the derived
assessment while retaining its provenance and historical evidence. This is a
local, paged view, not a global research summary or an accumulating conversation.

Within existing Fact navigation, the ordinary loop presents the latest registered
results of exposed Studies round-robin, then their earlier results. Complete
statements, original scopes, and actual predecessor references remain intact.
The ordering is provenance-based; it asserts neither strength nor relevance.
All earlier interfaces remain on forward pages. Object Cards and scheduler
selection rules are unchanged.

During INSPECT, one latest reading intent per exposed Study can survive further
pages. The final choice may continue, defer, or change those intentions. Absence
of an object's card on the current page does not make it mathematically inferior.
Reading intentions never pin a Study, advance a cursor, or become accepted Facts.
Final action/object-comparison rules apply only to ADVANCE/CONNECT/COMPOSE.
INSPECT still checks exposed Study ownership and legal, unrepeated material refs.

Research judgments share the existing attention ceiling and forward paging.
Oversized judgments get explicit source references, never truncated conditions.
Already read judgments yield space to a requested intact material page. The
ordinary local resolver may read a registered visit's `selection.json` as
unverified evidence, with the same packet-owner/scope guards as other visit
records. No arbitrary workspace file is thereby readable.

The selected assessment reaches Worker as `research_assessment_unverified` task
context. Only ordinary exact-scope material reads or a separately verified bridge
can supply `accepted_facts`; the Worker and fresh Verifier retain their existing
mathematical instructions and admission authority. Recovery reuses confirmed
selections and immutable page records. No new call, service counter, graph node,
or admission is created just to maintain the view.

Deterministic coverage is in `tests/test_research_progress.py`, alongside the
existing selection, material, bridge, recurrence, and research-delivery suites.
Live evaluation must assess changed actions and mathematical information, not
count assessments, aliases, or incremental Facts as capability improvement.
