# Unary representation recurrence

The continuous research entry checks an accepted Support before registering its
single new requirement as an independent Study. Multiple requirements, Supports
for another selected Claim, and reuse of an unrelated existing Claim are outside
this first slice. An exact ancestor duplicate remains the same existing Claim.

Before Support admission, the run freezes the new interface and one deterministic
root-to-selected-Claim path in the existing Support graph. Shared Claims use the
first stable path, never a union across branches. An independent local Claim has
only itself as an ancestor. No global graph or Fact proofs enter the probe.

A fresh probe can propose a possible representation equivalence. It has no truth
authority. One bridge Worker then proves both conditional directions; neither
input Claim is an accepted predecessor. This reuses the Fact bridge proof schema
and immutable receipts, CRPN recorded invocations, and closed-book Fact submission.
The fresh verifier additionally confirms both directions, identical ambient scope,
representation-only transport and absence of substantive new mathematics.
Only variable rename, finite relabelling, explicit definitions, notation and trivial
boundaries qualify. General equivalence, stronger theorems and AND decomposition
are not collapsed. This remains LLM verification, not a formal equivalence oracle.

The accepted equivalence Fact has no Claim predecessors and discharges neither
Claim. The original Support certificate and Claim stay immutable. A representation
record links the new Claim to its ancestor, certificate and original evidence.
It suppresses a new independent Study and its recurrence edge in effective search
depth. A requirement that already existed on the path records only the recurrence edge;
it never suppresses any original Study, even when the probe chooses another ancestor. The
originating Study receives feedback that the route returns to an ancestor-equivalent
claim and remains OPEN. No replacement strategy is prescribed.

Selector cards offer `representations:<ancestor-claim-id>` on demand. Reads are
paged, retain scope and full statement, and enter unverified research material,
never `accepted_facts`. The equivalence does not silently rebind a solved ancestor
Fact to another statement. Future proof reuse still needs a verified proof and
actual lineage through ordinary admission.

No-match, decline, failed checks, invalid output, timeout, overflow and interrupted
calls preserve normal Study creation. These extra roles use the same formal call
ledger and role attention limits: probe=Selector, transport=Worker, audit=Verifier.
They have no automatic retries. An unconfirmed call remains interrupted. Confirmed
responses and alias mutation are idempotent across recovery. Pauses/crashes retain
the pending stage. Revoking either certificate disables the representation and
restores an OPEN requirement's scheduling opportunity before the next selection.
The existing channel proportions and Worker mathematical prompt are unchanged.

Test entry: `python -m pytest -q tests/test_continuous_recurrence.py`.
Deterministic fixtures fix independent model judgements to test authority gates;
they are not evidence that an LLM recognizes every equivalent mathematical pair.
Real experiments remain isolated and their raw evidence stays outside Git.
