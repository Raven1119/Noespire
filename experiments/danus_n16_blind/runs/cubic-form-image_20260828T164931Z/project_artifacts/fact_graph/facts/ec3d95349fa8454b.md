## statement
Characterize the nonnegative integers representable in the form
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc
\]
with \(a,b,c\) nonnegative integers. Prove that a nonnegative integer \(N\) has such a representation if and only if either \(3\nmid N\) or \(9\mid N\).

## proof
Put
\[
S=a+b+c,\qquad Q=a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca.
\]
Direct multiplication gives the factorization
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=SQ. \tag{1}
\]
We first prove necessity. Suppose that the displayed cubic form represents N and that 3 divides N. For every integer u, one has u^3 congruent to u modulo 3. Reducing the represented value modulo 3 therefore gives
\[
0\equiv N\equiv a+b+c=S\pmod 3.
\]
Moreover,
\[
Q=S^2-3(ab+bc+ca).
\]
Since 3 divides S, this identity shows that 3 divides Q as well. Thus both factors on the right side of (1) are divisible by 3, so 9 divides N. Consequently, every represented N satisfies either 3 does not divide N or 9 divides N.

It remains to construct representations for all N satisfying that condition. The identity
\[
x^3+2y^3-3xy^2=(x-y)^2(x+2y) \tag{2}
\]
follows by expansion.

If N is congruent to 1 modulo 3, write N=3t+1 with t a nonnegative integer. Taking
\[
(a,b,c)=(t+1,t,t)
\]
and applying (2) with x=t+1 and y=t gives the value
\[
((t+1)-t)^2((t+1)+2t)=3t+1=N.
\]
If N is congruent to 2 modulo 3, write N=3t+2 with t a nonnegative integer. Taking
\[
(a,b,c)=(t,t+1,t+1)
\]
and applying (2) with x=t and y=t+1 gives
\[
(t-(t+1))^2(t+2(t+1))=3t+2=N.
\]
These two constructions cover every N not divisible by 3.

Now suppose 9 divides N. If N=0, the triple (0,0,0) is a representation. Otherwise write N=9m with m an integer at least 1, and take
\[
(a,b,c)=(m-1,m,m+1).
\]
All three entries are nonnegative. Their sum is S=3m. For this triple,
\[
a^2+b^2+c^2=3m^2+2
\]
and
\[
ab+bc+ca=3m^2-1,
\]
so Q=3. The factorization (1) now yields
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc=SQ=(3m)(3)=9m=N.
\]
Hence every nonnegative N satisfying either 3 does not divide N or 9 divides N is represented, completing both directions.

## intuition
The factorization separates the congruence obstruction into two factors: when the value is divisible by 3, both factors must be. For construction, two equal coordinates make the form linear in the remaining residue parameter, while three consecutive coordinates force the quadratic factor to equal 3.
