# Statement

For every positive integer n, the sum of the first n odd positive integers equals n^2.

# Proof

We prove the statement by induction on the positive integer n. The fact 5799bfde28efda3f gives the base case n=1. For the induction step, suppose the statement holds for a positive integer k. Then fact db836fa61119657a implies that it holds for k+1. Therefore, by mathematical induction, the statement holds for every positive integer n.
