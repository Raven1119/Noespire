# Statement

For every integer n, the integer n³ - n is divisible by 6.

# Proof

Let n be any integer and put m = n³ - n. The accepted divisible-by-2 predecessor proves that 2 divides m, and the accepted divisible-by-3 predecessor proves that 3 divides m. Since 2 and 3 are coprime, their product 6 divides m. Therefore n³ - n is divisible by 6.
