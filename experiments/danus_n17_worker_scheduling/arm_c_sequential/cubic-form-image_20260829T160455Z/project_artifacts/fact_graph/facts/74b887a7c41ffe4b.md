## statement
Characterize the nonnegative integers representable in the form
\[
a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc
\]
with \(a,b,c\) nonnegative integers. Prove that a nonnegative integer \(N\) has such a representation if and only if either \(3\nmid N\) or \(9\mid N\).

## proof
Let F(a,b,c)=a^3+b^3+c^3-3abc.

First prove necessity. For all integers a,b,c, direct expansion gives
F(a,b,c)=(a+b+c)Q(a,b,c),
where
Q(a,b,c)=a^2+b^2+c^2-ab-bc-ca.
Suppose N=F(a,b,c) for nonnegative integers a,b,c. For every integer x, the product x(x-1)(x+1) of three consecutive integers is divisible by 3, so x^3 is congruent to x modulo 3. Therefore
N=F(a,b,c) is congruent to a+b+c modulo 3.
If 3 divides N, it follows that 3 divides a+b+c. Furthermore,
Q(a,b,c)=(a+b+c)^2-3(ab+bc+ca).
Both terms on the right are divisible by 3 when 3 divides a+b+c, so 3 divides Q(a,b,c). Thus both factors a+b+c and Q(a,b,c) in the displayed factorization of F are divisible by 3. Hence 9 divides N. Consequently, every represented N satisfies either 3 does not divide N or 9 divides N.

Now prove sufficiency. Suppose N is a nonnegative integer satisfying either 3 does not divide N or 9 divides N.

If N is congruent to 1 modulo 3, then N=3t+1 for an integer t greater than or equal to 0. Choose (a,b,c)=(t+1,t,t). All three coordinates are nonnegative, and
F(t+1,t,t)=(t+1)^3+2t^3-3(t+1)t^2
=t^3+3t^2+3t+1+2t^3-3t^3-3t^2
=3t+1=N.

If N is congruent to 2 modulo 3, then N=3t+2 for an integer t greater than or equal to 0. Choose (a,b,c)=(t,t+1,t+1). All three coordinates are nonnegative, and
F(t,t+1,t+1)=t^3+2(t+1)^3-3t(t+1)^2
=3t^3+6t^2+6t+2-(3t^3+6t^2+3t)
=3t+2=N.

It remains to consider 9 dividing N. If N=0, choose (a,b,c)=(0,0,0), which gives F(a,b,c)=0. If N is positive, write N=9t for an integer t greater than or equal to 1. Choose (a,b,c)=(t+1,t,t-1). These coordinates are nonnegative, and
F(t+1,t,t-1)=(t+1)^3+t^3+(t-1)^3-3(t+1)t(t-1)
=(3t^3+6t)-(3t^3-3t)
=9t=N.

The cases above represent every nonnegative N satisfying the stated divisibility condition, and the necessity argument excludes precisely the nonnegative integers divisible by 3 but not by 9. This proves the characterization.

## intuition
The form factors into the sum a+b+c and a quadratic factor. Divisibility by 3 forces both factors to be divisible by 3, producing the obstruction modulo 9. Three simple neighboring-coordinate families attain every permitted congruence class.
