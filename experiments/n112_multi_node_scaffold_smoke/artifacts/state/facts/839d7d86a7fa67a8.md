# Statement

Prove that for every positive integer n, the sum of the first n positive odd integers is n^2.

# Proof

We use mathematical induction on n. The statement holds for n = 1 by Fact c01d8d0ce1716e1e. Now let k be a positive integer and assume that the sum of the first k positive odd integers is k^2. By Fact f77b1e0364de7558, the sum of the first k + 1 positive odd integers is then (k + 1)^2. Therefore, by induction, for every positive integer n, the sum of the first n positive odd integers is n^2.
