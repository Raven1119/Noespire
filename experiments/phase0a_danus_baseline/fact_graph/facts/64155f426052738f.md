# Statement

The sum of the first four positive odd integers is 16: 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 16.

# Proof

By the accepted fact, 1 + 3 + 5 = 9. Adding 7 gives 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 9 + 7 = 16.
